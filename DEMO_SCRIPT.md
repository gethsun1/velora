# Velora client demonstration

1. Open the premium homepage and establish Eldoret coverage.
2. Explore the curated packages and flavour edit.
3. Configure a Birthday Lounge experience and show the itemized estimate.
4. Submit guest details, consent and pay-on-delivery selection.
5. Show the booking reference and tracking timeline.
6. Sign in through `/admin/login` with the demo administrator.
7. Review live operational KPIs and the new order.
8. Confirm the booking, reserve an available asset and assign delivery staff.
9. Mark the order out for delivery and return to customer tracking.
10. Show persistent inventory and explain planned Daraja and WhatsApp provider adapters.
